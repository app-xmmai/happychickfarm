Install XMM AI on your phone

Add XMM AI to your home screen for full-screen, faster access. It takes less than a minute and needs no app store.

1
Open XMM AI in Safari
2
Tap the Share buttonThe square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen on iPhone or at the top on iPad.
3
Tap “Add to Home Screen”Scroll down in the share menu until you see it.
4
Tap “Add”XMM AI now appears on your Home Screen and opens like a normal app.
1
Open XMM AI in Chrome
2
Tap the menuThe three dots in the top-right corner of Chrome.
3
Tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”Which one you see depends on your Chrome version.
4
Tap “Install”Open XMM AI from your home screen or app drawer.
